Your loan gets a second look. And a third.

Most lenders disappear after closing. We treat every mortgage as one chapter in a longer financial story, and keep reading. First-time buyer or seasoned multi-property owner, the process is similar. The strategy is what changes.

  1. A real conversation about where you are, where you want to be in five years, and what the house is supposed to do for you.

  2. Program, term, down payment and rate strategy chosen to fit the plan, rather than whatever is easiest to close.

  3. Plain-English updates at every milestone, and a team that picks up the phone whenever something comes up.

  4. An annual check-in on rates, equity and goals. Refinance, tap equity, buy the next property, or simply stay the course.

Loan programs

  • 30-Year FixedThe steady, predictable payment

    One rate and one principal-and-interest payment for the life of the loan. The most popular choice for buyers who plan to stay put and want a budget that never surprises them.

  • 15-Year FixedOwn it outright, sooner

    A higher monthly payment in exchange for a typically lower rate and far less interest paid overall. A strong refinance move when income has grown.

  • Adjustable-RateLower start, planned exit

    An introductory fixed period followed by periodic adjustments. Useful when you expect to sell, relocate or refinance within a known window.

  • VA LoansFor those who served

    Zero down payment options and no monthly mortgage insurance for eligible veterans, active-duty service members and surviving spouses. Our team walks you through every nuance.

  • FHA LoansFlexible credit, low down payment

    Government-backed financing with down payments as low as 3.5% and more flexible credit guidelines. A common path for first-time buyers.

  • Jumbo LoansAbove conforming limits

    Financing for higher-priced homes, which in coastal San Diego County is often simply the market. Structured for strong borrowers with larger loan amounts.

  • USDA LoansZero down in eligible areas

    No-down-payment financing for qualifying buyers in eligible rural and suburban areas, with income limits that vary by county.

  • Renovation (203k)Buy it and fix it, one loan

    Roll the purchase price and the cost of improvements into a single mortgage, so the house you buy can become the house you want.

  • Reverse MortgageTurn equity into income

    For homeowners 62 and older, convert part of your home equity into cash with no required monthly mortgage payment while you live in the home and meet loan obligations.
